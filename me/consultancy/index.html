Done-For-You Services

We bring our philosophy to the practical work of building sites that serve our social network: accessible, light, secure and respectful of the people who use them.

If you prefer to focus on what you do best, we offer full-service website design and deployment. The consultancy is broader than web design: we assess and audit accessibility (WCAG / AODA), page weight and speed, security hygiene, and the site's ecological impact, meaning its footprint on the social network and the wider web (trackers, data shared with platforms, bloat and load on visitors' devices and networks). Each is weighed only to the degree it will actually help a client. Using an AI-assisted co-pilot model, we eliminate time-and-materials risk for small businesses, independent writers, and owner-operated brands. We handle the technical setup including legitimate search engine optimization so you can focus on content and connecting with your community however you define that term.

  • No Page Limits: You supply the content; we build as many pages as you need initially and train you on content management.

  • Complete Package: Includes an intake call via video, full site build, one round of revisions, a plain-English user guide, live training, and a 30-day fix warranty.

  • True Ownership & Security: Your site sits securely on your local device with static pages (no database, login screens, or vulnerable plugins exposed). You retain full ownership of your domain and accounts. We hold no standing access; you hold the keys and only grant access when you want support.
